# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is hiring teams evaluating Meet Mehta for software engineering roles. They need to understand his focus, technical range, project experience, and contact options quickly enough to decide whether to interview him.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Meet's background, selected work, capabilities, internship experience, credentials, and contact details. Success means helping a hiring team reach a confident interview decision.

## Positioning

Meet works across product interfaces and full-stack implementation, with projects grounded in operational workflows such as food redistribution, interview preparation, clinical operations, and placement management.

## Operating Context

Visitors are likely scanning the site during candidate review on desktop or mobile. Project details, live links, credentials, downloadable resume, and direct contact actions support that evaluation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve all verified biography, education, project, credential, contact, and destination-link facts from the existing implementation.
- The site is a responsive Next.js web application.
- Existing project-detail, theme, sound, contact-copy, and navigation behavior should remain functional unless a redesigned interaction replaces it cleanly.
- Factual claims must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

Use only sans-serif typography. The portfolio should be simple and elegant, with enough personality to avoid generic developer-portfolio styling. shadcn/ui components may be used when they fit the design.

## Evidence on Hand

- Portrait photography at `public/portrait.jpg` and `public/about.jpg`.
- Resume at `public/resume.pdf`.
- Project facts and links in `src/data/projects.ts`.
- Biography, education, internship, certification, and contact facts in `src/app/page.tsx`.
- No testimonials, employer endorsements, product metrics, or press evidence are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Let project evidence lead the hiring decision.
- Make the page fast to scan without flattening the work into a skills list.
- Keep every factual claim traceable to existing content or assets.
- Express personality through composition and craft, not decorative technology tropes.
- Keep primary actions obvious on desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and responsive behavior.
